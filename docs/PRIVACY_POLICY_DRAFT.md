JUPITAR processes typed messages, microphone utterances and approved one-shot
screenshots locally through bundled inference components. No cloud inference,
analytics, remote fonts or automatic conversation upload is included. Explicit
model downloads contact Hugging Face and its storage providers. Browser searches,
mail drafts and support links open external apps with their own privacy behavior.

Nearby speech is locally processed before wake matching after first-run consent.
No audio recordings, chat history, screenshots or native engine logs are deliberately
saved by this app. OS paging and crash facilities may retain process data.
Approved memories, notes, reminders and settings are unencrypted local JSON in
%LOCALAPPDATA%/JUPITAR. Workspace documents and downloaded models remain locally.
Memory UI supports edit/delete. Clear chat clears session context, not approved
memory. Uninstall preserves user data; users may delete the data folder when the
app is closed after backing up wanted files. No automatic purchases or payments.

Before publishing, add contact/date/URL, verify these statements against the final
binary, assess applicable laws and age-related requirements, and disclose any
later telemetry, online services or retention changes. This draft is not legal advice.
